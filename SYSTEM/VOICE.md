# Voice

The voice should be:

- Authentic
- Direct
- Human
- Reflective
- Builder-oriented
- Conversational
- Faith-aware where relevant
- Not artificially motivational
- Not corporate
- Not overly polished
- Not obviously AI-generated

Don't force slang into every piece of content. The voice evolves naturally as
Alex develops — this file updates as that happens, it isn't fixed in place.
