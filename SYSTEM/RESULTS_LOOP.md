# Results Loop

Records what happens after content is published and turns useful results
into future content decisions. Approved results are stored in
`CONTENT/RESULTS.md`.

## Flow

```
PUBLISHED → RESULT → OBSERVATION → LESSON → REVISION → FUTURE CONTENT
```

## Result Record

```
ID:
CONTENT_ID:
DATE:
PLATFORM:
RESULT:
OBSERVATION:
AUDIENCE_RESPONSE:
LESSON:
REVISION:
NEXT_ACTION:
STATUS:
```

`CONTENT_ID` links back to the format/opportunity that was actually
published (`CONTENT/FORMATS.md` FMT-### or `CONTENT/EPISODES.md` EP-###).

## Result Types

`POSITIVE` · `NEGATIVE` · `MIXED` · `UNKNOWN`

Views/likes are not automatic success or failure signals on their own.
Capture qualitative signals too: comments, questions, DMs, objections,
conversations, leads, client interest, repeated topics, unexpected reactions.

## Revision Rule

A result can modify: future hook, format, topic angle, audience targeting,
delivery, or series priority.

A result must **not** rewrite the original story or fact it came from — the
source record (capture/story/lesson) stays as documented. Revision changes
how the idea is delivered next time, not what actually happened.

## What Doesn't Get Logged

No fabricated metrics, no fake results. If a piece was published but the
outcome isn't known or wasn't tracked, that's `RESULT: UNKNOWN` — not a
guess. Nothing gets a result record until it's actually published; nothing
in this repo has been published yet, so `CONTENT/RESULTS.md` currently holds
no entries.

## Not Yet Automated

No analytics integrations, no automation. Results are logged manually after
Alex reports what actually happened.
