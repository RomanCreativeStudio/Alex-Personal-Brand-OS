# Content Rules

1. Authenticity over virality.
2. Real experience over manufactured stories.
3. Story before polish.
4. Value over empty motivation.
5. Document the journey.
6. Questions can become content opportunities.
7. Not every question becomes content.
8. AI can organize and develop ideas but cannot invent Alex's experiences.
9. Personal faith/value content must represent Alex accurately.
10. Business claims must be factual.
11. AIMA development must remain technically and historically accurate.
12. Preserve the evolution of ideas rather than rewriting history.
