# Capture Protocol

## Commands

**CAPTURE** — Capture the current moment/conversation as structured
information (ID, date, source, core realization, status → `CONTENT/IDEAS.md`).

**STORY** — Determine whether the information belongs in Alex's larger
personal story (→ `STORY/MASTER_STORY.md`, `TIMELINE.md`, or `KEY_MOMENTS.md`).

**EPISODE** — Develop a captured idea into an episode concept (→
`CONTENT/EPISODES.md`). A concept is not a finalized script.

**LESSON** — Extract the lesson Alex is learning from an experience (→
`CONTENT/LESSONS.md`).

**UPDATE OS** — Identify which OS files should be updated based on new
information, and update only those.

**REVISE** — Update an existing idea/entry based on new information. Preserve
the evolution of the idea rather than overwriting its history (see
`CONTENT_RULES.md`, rule 12).

## Classification

Every captured item can be classified as one or more of:

`PRIVATE` · `STORY` · `LESSON` · `CONTENT IDEA` · `EPISODE` · `BUSINESS` ·
`AIMA` · `RCS` · `FUTURE`

Not every conversation becomes content. Distinguish between something Alex
experienced, something he learned, a potential content angle, a finalized
episode, and something that should stay private.

## Missing Information

Never fabricate missing information. When Alex's actual experience/details are
required and not yet provided, mark the field `NEEDS_INPUT`.
