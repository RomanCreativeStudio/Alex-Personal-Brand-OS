# CTA Templates

Use only when the CTA fits the story. Do not force a CTA into every episode.

## Follow

“Follow along—I’m documenting the full 90-day journey.”

## Next Episode

“I’ll show you what happens next.”

## Conversation

“Would you have handled this differently?”

## Business

“I'm building Roman Creative Studio in public and tracking the real numbers.”

## Rule

The story comes first. The CTA should take one sentence or less.
