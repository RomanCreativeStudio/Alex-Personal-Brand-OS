# 90-Day Journey — RCS Content Repurposing Engine

## Purpose

Turn each piece of the 90-Day Journey into multiple useful pieces of content without multiplying production time.

The original RCS repurposing system establishes the rule: content should not be single-use. One core idea can be adapted into different formats for different platforms. fileciteturn17file0

## Core Flow

```text
One Journey Story
  ├─ TikTok
  ├─ Instagram Reel
  ├─ YouTube Short
  └─ LinkedIn post (when appropriate)
```

## Example

### Source Story
"I contacted 20 businesses this week and only got 2 responses."

### TikTok / Reel
Hook → what happened → what I learned → what I will change next week.

### YouTube Short
Same core video, adjusted title/caption for search and discovery.

### LinkedIn
A professional lesson about outreach, sales, or building a service business.

## Repurposing Rules

1. Adapt; do not blindly copy captions.
2. Keep the underlying story consistent.
3. Make each platform feel native.
4. Never manufacture a result for a better story.
5. Do not repurpose every video everywhere if it adds work without meaningful benefit.
6. Prioritize TikTok and Instagram Reels first.
7. Repurpose to YouTube Shorts second.
8. Use LinkedIn selectively for strong business lessons.

## Weekly Production Method

```text
Monday–Friday
  ↓
Capture real journey moments
  ↓
Choose the strongest story of the day
  ↓
Turn it into one primary short-form video
  ↓
Publish to TikTok + Instagram Reels
  ↓
Repurpose to YouTube Shorts when appropriate
  ↓
Convert strong business lessons into LinkedIn posts
```

## Efficiency Target

The goal is not maximum posting volume. The goal is maximum storytelling value from the work already being done.

A good week should produce approximately 5 primary videos without requiring hours of separate content creation every day.
