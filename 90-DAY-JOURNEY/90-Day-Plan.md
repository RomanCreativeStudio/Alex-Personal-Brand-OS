# 90-Day Journey — RCS Plan

## Mission

Document the real attempt to generate **$10,000 in RCS revenue in 90 days** while building Roman Creative Studio.

This is a business-building documentary, not a manufactured challenge. The content must reflect what actually happens.

## Rules

1. Day 1 begins when the first official journey content is recorded/published.
2. Goal: $10,000 in collected RCS revenue during the 90-day period.
3. Track revenue, clients, leads, meetings, proposals, and completed projects.
4. Never fabricate results.
5. Saturday and Sunday are film-free.
6. Content should document work already being done, not create extra work unnecessarily.
7. Keep editing minimal so the majority of time stays on RCS, clients, outreach, and delivery.
8. Five primary videos per week is the default target; quality and consistency matter more than volume.

## 13-Week Structure

| Phase | Weeks | Focus |
|---|---:|---|
| Foundation | 1–2 | Establish the story, systems, outreach, first wins |
| Traction | 3–5 | Increase outreach, meetings, proposals, projects |
| Delivery | 6–8 | Deliver client work while continuing sales |
| Growth | 9–11 | Improve close rate, referrals, positioning |
| Final Push | 12–13 | Close the gap to $10k and finish the story |
