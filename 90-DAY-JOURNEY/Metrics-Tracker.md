## Content Metrics

Track these only after publishing:

- Views
- Average watch time when available
- Likes
- Comments
- Shares
- Saves
- Followers gained
- Profile visits
- Leads generated from content

Content metrics are diagnostic. They do not replace the $10,000 revenue scoreboard.
