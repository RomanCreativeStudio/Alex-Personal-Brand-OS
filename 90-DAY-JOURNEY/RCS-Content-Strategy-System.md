# 90-Day Journey — RCS Content Strategy System

## Purpose

This is the personal-brand adaptation of the Roman Creative Studio content strategy system for Alexander Roman's 90-Day Journey: documenting the attempt to generate $10,000 in RCS revenue in 90 days.

This file is intentionally adapted rather than copied wholesale. The original RCS agency system defines content for the agency itself; this system is for documenting the founder's real journey while building RCS.

## Core Content Pillars

### 1. Mission
Show the goal, stakes, decisions, and what Alexander is trying to accomplish.

Examples:
- Why I'm giving myself 90 days to make $10,000 with RCS
- What I need to accomplish this week
- The plan for getting the next client

### 2. Work
Document the actual work being done.

Examples:
- Building a client website
- Prospect research
- Sending outreach
- Improving the RCS website
- Creating proposals
- Building systems

### 3. Reality
Show the honest process, including setbacks and uncertainty.

Examples:
- A client said no
- Outreach did not perform
- Something broke
- A week did not go according to plan
- Balancing work and RCS

### 4. Progress
Show measurable movement.

Track real numbers only:
- Revenue
- Clients
- Leads contacted
- Meetings
- Proposals
- Projects completed

Never fabricate results or metrics.

### 5. Weekly Recap
Every Friday, summarize the week and update the audience on the 90-day goal.

Structure:
- What happened
- What worked
- What failed
- Numbers
- Lesson learned
- Next week's target

## Weekly Publishing System

The core production schedule is Monday–Friday because Saturday and Sunday are film-free.

| Day | Content | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Mission | Set the week's objective |
| Tuesday | Work | Show execution |
| Wednesday | Reality | Show the honest process |
| Thursday | Progress | Show measurable movement |
| Friday | Weekly Recap | Close the week and update the score |

Target: 5 primary short-form videos per week, approximately 65 primary videos across 13 weeks. Additional repurposed posts are optional and should not create unnecessary production burden.

## Platform Priority

Primary:
- TikTok
- Instagram Reels

Secondary/repurposed:
- YouTube Shorts
- LinkedIn when the content naturally fits

The same core story can be adapted across platforms, but captions, hooks, and presentation should remain platform-native.

## Content Rules

1. One clear idea per video.
2. Hook immediately; do not begin with filler greetings.
3. Document real work rather than pretending to have results that do not exist.
4. Show process, not just finished websites.
5. Use specific numbers when they are real.
6. Let setbacks remain part of the story.
7. Keep the content connected to the $10,000 / 90-day objective.
8. Do not turn every post into a sales pitch.
9. Keep production fast enough that content does not interfere with building RCS.
10. Maintain a consistent visual identity.

## Repurposing Principle

Create the core story once, then adapt it where useful:

```text
Primary short-form video
  ├─ Instagram Reel
  ├─ TikTok
  ├─ YouTube Short
  └─ LinkedIn post (only when the story has professional/B2B value)
```
