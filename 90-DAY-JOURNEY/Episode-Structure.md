# Episode Structure

## Standard Episode

### 1. Hook — 0–3 seconds
State the tension, goal, result, or question.

Examples:
- “I’m trying to make $10,000 in 90 days with a web-design business.”
- “I just got another rejection.”
- “This is the first client project of the challenge.”

### 2. Context — 3–10 seconds
Explain what is happening without overexplaining.

### 3. Proof — 10–40 seconds
Show the actual work, screen, message, meeting, build, result, or obstacle.

### 4. Outcome — 40–55 seconds
Say what happened.

### 5. Next Move — final seconds
Give the viewer a reason to follow the next episode.

## Fast Version

For simple updates:

**Hook → Proof → Result → Next move**

## Rules

- One main story per video.
- Use real footage whenever possible.
- Keep numbers accurate.
- Do not force a lesson into a moment that does not have one.
- Do not add unnecessary cinematic effects.
- End with forward momentum.

## Voice

Conversational, honest, ambitious, grounded, and professional. The story should feel like someone watching the business being built in real time—not a commercial pretending everything is perfect.
