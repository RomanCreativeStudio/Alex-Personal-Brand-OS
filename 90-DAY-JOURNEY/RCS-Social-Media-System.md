# 90-Day Journey — RCS Social Media System

## Purpose

This is the personal-brand version of the RCS social-media strategy for documenting Alexander Roman's 90-Day Journey while building Roman Creative Studio.

The original RCS social system treats social media as an authority and trust engine rather than posting for vanity metrics. That principle remains, but the personal-brand version centers the founder's real-time journey. fileciteturn18file0

## Platform Priority

| Platform | Priority | Role |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | Primary | Discovery + journey storytelling |
| Instagram Reels | Primary | Personal brand + visual storytelling |
| YouTube Shorts | Secondary | Repurposed short-form discovery |
| LinkedIn | Selective | Business lessons + professional authority |

## Video Style

- Vertical 9:16
- Short-form first
- Hook in the first 1–2 seconds
- Captions on screen
- Real footage of the work
- Screen recordings when useful
- Talking-head clips when useful
- Behind-the-scenes footage
- Website/design reveals
- No filler intros

## Content Mix

### Mission
What am I trying to accomplish?

### Work
What am I actually doing today?

### Reality
What went wrong, what surprised me, or what is difficult?

### Progress
What changed numerically or materially?

### Recap
What did this week teach me and what happens next?

## Platform Adaptation

### TikTok
Prioritize strong hooks, fast pacing, honest storytelling, and useful business lessons.

### Instagram Reels
Use the same core story with stronger visual presentation and clean captions.

### YouTube Shorts
Reuse the strongest short-form videos and make the title clear and searchable where appropriate.

### LinkedIn
Only repurpose stories that contain a useful business, sales, marketing, entrepreneurship, or web-design lesson.

## Authority Rules

1. Show the process, not only polished results.
2. Use real numbers.
3. Never fabricate client results, revenue, leads, or milestones.
4. Do not make every post a pitch.
5. Let the audience see both wins and setbacks.
6. Stay specific instead of making generic entrepreneurship content.
7. Keep the $10,000 / 90-day goal visible throughout the story.

## Publishing Rule

Consistency matters more than trying to post everywhere every day. The original RCS system emphasizes sustainable consistency over bursts of volume. fileciteturn18file0

For this journey, the operating target is:

**5 primary videos per week, Monday–Friday.**

Saturday and Sunday remain film-free.
