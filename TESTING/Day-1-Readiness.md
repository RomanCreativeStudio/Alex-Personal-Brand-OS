# Day 1 Readiness

## Do Not Overbuild

Day 1 should start as soon as the basic workflow works. The system does not need to be perfect.

## Ready Checklist

- [ ] 90-day goal is defined: $10,000 RCS revenue.
- [ ] Starting revenue is recorded.
- [ ] Day 1 date is recorded.
- [ ] Phone/camera is ready.
- [ ] Master editing template is available.
- [ ] Captions work.
- [ ] Export works.
- [ ] Publishing accounts are ready.
- [ ] Metrics tracker is ready.
- [ ] First story is identified.

## Day 1 Recording

Record a simple 30–60 second introduction:

1. What the challenge is.
2. Why you are doing it.
3. What Roman Creative Studio is.
4. The $10,000 target.
5. What you are doing today.

Then capture the actual work.

## Day 1 Editing

Use the minimum-edit workflow. Do not create a special cinematic production unless the footage naturally calls for it.

## Day 1 Success

Day 1 is successful if the first official episode is captured, edited, published/queued, and the real starting numbers are logged.

The objective is to begin the journey—not to perfect the system forever.
