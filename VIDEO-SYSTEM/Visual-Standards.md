# Visual Standards

## Locked Standards

- Canvas: 1080 × 1920
- Frame rate: 30 FPS
- Font: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Format: vertical short-form video
- Baseline color grade: use the established preset/settings

## Style

Modern, clean, documentary, professional, and personal.

The visual identity should support the story without making every episode feel like an advertisement.

## Text

Use one clear hook/title at a time. Keep text readable on a phone and avoid filling the screen with information.

## B-Roll

Prioritize footage already captured during real work:

- Laptop/desktop
- Websites
- Design/build process
- Outreach
- Meetings
- Workspace
- Relevant real-life moments

## Consistency Rule

Once the standards are working, **lock them for the 90 days**. Do not spend productive business time experimenting with fonts, colors, layouts, or effects.

## Quality Bar

Clear > cinematic.

Authentic > staged.

Consistent > complicated.

Published > endlessly polished.
