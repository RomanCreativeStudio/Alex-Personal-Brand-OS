# Master Intro Montage Blueprint

## Project Specs

- Resolution: 1080 × 1920
- Aspect ratio: 9:16
- Frame rate: 30 FPS
- Runtime: 7.5 seconds
- Font: Plus Jakarta Sans

## Purpose

A reusable 7.5-second identity intro for the 90-Day Journey. Build it once and reuse it rather than recreating an intro for every episode.

## Timeline

**0.0–1.5s — Identity**

Quick establishing shot of Alexander/RCS work.

**1.5–3.0s — Build**

Fast cut showing website/design/business activity.

**3.0–5.0s — Journey**

Visual cue for the $10,000 / 90-day challenge.

**5.0–6.5s — Brand**

Roman Creative Studio / personal-brand identifier.

**6.5–7.5s — Transition**

Clean transition into the episode's actual story.

## Reuse Rule

The montage is a **master asset**. Do not edit its structure for ordinary episodes.

If the intro ever becomes repetitive, test a version without it rather than spending time rebuilding it every episode.

## Visual Standards

- Clean
- Modern
- Fast but readable
- Minimal effects
- Consistent typography
- Consistent baseline grade
- No unnecessary transitions

## Editing Principle

The montage exists to establish identity once. The episode itself carries the story.
