# CapCut Workflow

## Role

CapCut is the fast-edit option for ordinary 90-Day Journey episodes.

## Workflow

1. Open the reusable project/template.
2. Import the day's main clip.
3. Trim dead space.
4. Generate auto captions.
5. Apply the saved text/font treatment.
6. Add 1–3 supporting clips if needed.
7. Apply the saved baseline adjustment.
8. Check audio/captions.
9. Export 1080 × 1920 at 30 FPS.

## Time Target

**10–20 minutes maximum per normal episode.**

## Reuse

Keep one master template. Duplicate it for each episode.

Do not redesign the template during the 90-day challenge unless a real problem is slowing production.

## Fast-Edit Rule

If the video communicates the story clearly, publish it. Do not keep polishing for marginal improvements.
